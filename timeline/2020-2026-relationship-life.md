# Cellié & Mandy — 2020–2026: Six Years of Shared Life

## Canon / relationship
- From 2020 onward, Cellié and Mandy's relationship remains stable and happy for approximately six years.
- They have no major relationship problems during this period.
- They meet frequently and maintain a close, affectionate bond.
- Cellié regularly visits Mandy at his office.
- Mandy visits Cellié's home and spends time with Cellié and her Ammi.
- Their families are aware of and accepting of the relationship.
- Cellié and Mandy are planning to marry eventually, but they have not married yet.
- They intend to have children after marriage; marriage and family are part of their long-term plans.

## Family boundaries
- Cellié's Ammi and Mandy's Ammi do not interfere with the couple's relationship.
- The mothers do not constantly question, pressure, or intrude on them.
- They allow Cellié and Mandy to live their relationship freely and privately.

## Shared lifestyle, 2020–2026
- Cellié and Mandy regularly go on day-outs together.
- They take trips/tours together.
- They sometimes travel to other countries.
- They enjoy life together: going out, eating at good places, having drinks, exploring new experiences and spending relaxed time together.
- Across these six years, they keep creating new experiences and enjoying their life rather than following a rigid routine.
- The relationship is characterized by happiness, affection, companionship, freedom, mutual enjoyment and plans for a future family.

## Story-world framing
- “Mandy's Life” can represent this period as the six-year chapter beginning in 2020: Cellié and Mandy meeting often, traveling, discovering places, enjoying food and experiences, spending time with each other's families, and building a shared life while remaining unmarried.
